How I work

Verifiable contracts: typed result models, explicit verifiers, and source-bound claims rather than unchecked trust in serialized values.

Adversarial evidence: empty, duplicate, partial, boundary, and forged inputs are treated as first-class regression cases.

Lean formalization: research-level statements in Lean 4 / mathlib, with source fidelity, category and AMS metadata, and lake --wfail validation.

Benchmark quality: statement correctness and source fidelity for AI4Math evaluation, including separating open problems from refuted strengthenings.
